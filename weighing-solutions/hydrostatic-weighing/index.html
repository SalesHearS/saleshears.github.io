Technology

Hydrostatic weighing explained

A vessel rests on fluid-filled load cells. The weight becomes fluid pressure, carried by tubing to a totalizer in a clean area. No electronics at the weighing site.

Request a quoteTank and vessel weighing

How it works

The only parts at the vessel are mechanical: the load cells and the tubing. The signal is created away from the washdown area.

From load to reading

  1. 01

    Weight loads the cell

    The vessel and its contents press on each load cell.

  2. 02

    Pressure builds

    The load changes the pressure of the fluid inside the cell.

  3. 03

    Tubing carries it

    Tubing carries the pressure to the totalizer in a clean area.

  4. 04

    The indicator shows weight

    The totalizer converts the pressure and the indicator shows the weight.

Why it suits wet and corrosive areas

Because the load cells and tubing are mechanical, there are no cables, junction boxes or electronics to protect at the vessel. That is why hydrostatic weighing is often considered for washdown, corrosive and hard-to-reach locations.

  • No electronics at the weighing site
  • The signal is created in a clean area
  • Materials can be chosen for the cleaning regime

Frequently asked questions

What is a hydrostatic load cell?

A load cell that holds fluid in a sealed chamber. Load on the cell changes the fluid pressure, which tubing carries to a totalizer.

Is electricity needed at the vessel?

No. The load cells and tubing work purely by mechanics and fluid pressure. The pressure only becomes an electrical signal in the totalizer.

How far can the totalizer be from the vessel?

Within the tubing length allowed by Emery Winslow Scale. We confirm the length for each system.

Is approval for trade use available?

Approvals depend on the product and the country. We confirm the approvals that apply in Vietnam before quoting a system for trade use.
