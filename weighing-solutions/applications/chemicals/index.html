Application

Weighing in chemical plants

Corrosive atmospheres and demanding areas are hard on cables and electronics. We help you choose a weighing approach that suits them.

Request a quoteHydrostatic weighing

The weighing challenges

  • Corrosive atmosphere

    Vapours and spills attack cables, connectors and enclosures over time.

  • Cleaning and process chemicals

    Materials must suit both the process and the cleaning routine.

  • Area requirements

    Hazardous-area rules must be defined early. Any approval wording is confirmed from documents for the specific product.

Choosing materials and layout

The right material depends on the chemical, the concentration and the temperature. We check these with you and with the manufacturer.

  • Stainless steels and polymers for wetted and exposed parts
  • Few parts at the vessel, and the display placed away from the area
  • Containment, drainage and access for maintenance

Information to prepare

  • The chemicals in the vessel and around it
  • Concentration and operating temperature
  • Capacity range and the resolution you need
  • Area classification and any approval requirements
  • Cleaning routine and spill containment
  • Required signal to your control system
