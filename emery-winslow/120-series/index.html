120 Series LifeMount tank and vessel weighing system

Light and medium capacity hydrostatic weighing for tanks, hoppers, mixers and blenders from 1,500 to 18,000 lb (about 0.7 to 8.2 tonnes).

  • 3.88 in (99 mm) low profile

Why Emery Winslow hydrostatic

  • No electronics at the scale

    Hydrostatic load cells turn weight into fluid pressure. Stainless tubing carries it to a totalizer in a clean, dry area; only there does it become an electronic signal.

  • Made for water, washdown, lightning and surges

    The manufacturer lists water, high-pressure washdown, lightning, power surges, rodents and RFI/EMI among the causes of failure its load cells are designed to withstand.

  • Stainless steel construction

    Stainless steel load cells (304, with 316 available for the 136 Series) and 316 stainless tubing for corrosive and sanitary areas.

  • Made in the USA, roots in 1868

    Roots in the A.H. Emery Company, founded in 1868; joined with Winslow (founded 1896) in 1974. Built at the Seymour, Connecticut manufacturing center.

Strengths as stated by Emery Winslow Scale Company: Hydrostatic Weighing Systems brochure (06-15-2026), 120 and 136 Series sheets, and emerywinslow.com/about.

Small vessels, harsh rooms

The 120 Series uses the same hydrostatic principle as the 136 Series at lower capacities. The load cells are self-checking, which the manufacturer recommends for mixers and agitated weighing; an external checking mechanism is optional for severe agitation and vibration.

All electronics can be located away from the weighing site and washdown areas. The manufacturer lists the system for flammable or explosive environments and for chemical rooms where caustic cleaning agents are used.

Each system includes

  • 120S stainless steel hydrostatic load cells
  • Totalizer in an N4X enclosure
  • 316 stainless steel tubing
  • Installation and maintenance manual

Applications named by the manufacturer

  • Systems with total capacities of 1,500 to 18,000 lb
  • 304 stainless steel with IP69K protection for repeated heavy washdown and sanitation
  • Mixers and agitated weighing (self-checking cells)
  • Flammable or explosive environments
  • Tanks, hoppers, mixers, blenders, grinders and all types of vessels
  • Conveyor scales, silos, bins and custom-engineered designs

Per the manufacturer, the product is used in: meat, poultry and seafood; chemicals, solvents and acids; food; pharmaceuticals and nutraceuticals; aerospace, defense and munitions; distilleries; paper, printing, ink and paint; and many other process industries.

Options for process control

Pair the load cells with an Emery Winslow 7600E or 7800 MAX indicator for a complete system.

  • Ethernet TCP, EtherNet/IP and analog output
  • Setpoint relays (DIO) for process control
  • Tool kit, load and base plates
  • Anchor bolts with leveling nuts (confirmed by HearS for each project)
