Emery Winslow case study · Aviation · weight and balance

El Dorado Airport, Bogotá: weight and balance checks up to a Boeing 747

A custom aircraft weighing system, developed with the International Civil Aviation Organization.

All case studies

What happened

In 1999 Emery Winslow developed what it describes as the first Automatic Aircraft Weight and Balance Verification System (AAWBVS), working with the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO).

The system is installed at El Dorado International Airport in Bogotá, Colombia, and can verify the weight and balance of a full range of aircraft up to and including the Boeing 747.

Benefits listed by the manufacturer: improved safety, fuel economy and loading efficiency, with reduced maintenance, noise and pollution.

  • Demand: weight and balance of the largest passenger aircraft
  • Built: custom verification system, 1999
  • Shows: custom engineering well beyond standard scales

Source: Emery Winslow Scale Company, “Boeing 747: Automatic Aircraft Weight and Balance Verification System”, https://www.emerywinslow.com/boeing-747/, accessed 15/11/2026. Read the original case study.
