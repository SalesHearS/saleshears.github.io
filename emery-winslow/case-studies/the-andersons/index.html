The Andersons: a 70-foot hydrostatic truck scale since 1986

A grain business that saw a hydrostatic truck scale at another plant, installed its own in 1986 and then used hydrostatic scales wherever it could.

All case studies

What happened

In the mid-1980s staff from The Andersons visited an Emery Winslow hydrostatic scale at a Campbell Soup tomato processing plant in Napoleon, Ohio. In 1986 the company installed a 70-foot (about 21 m) Emery Winslow truck scale at its grain elevator in Findlay, Ohio.

The case study explains that the hydrostatic cells are non-electric, carry a manufacturer guarantee against water and electrical damage including lightning, and that there are no electric cables under the scale for rodents to damage.

“With our Hydrostatic scales, we’ve significantly reduced the amount of routine maintenance needed.” “We liked it well enough that we’ve gone with Hydrostatic scales anywhere it’s feasible.” David Myles, The Andersons Inc.

  • Harsh factor: outdoor weighbridge, lightning, water, rodents
  • Installed: 70 ft (about 21 m) truck scale, 1986
  • Reported: significantly less routine maintenance

Source: Emery Winslow Scale Company, “The Andersons: Ecstatic for Hydrostatic”, https://www.emerywinslow.com/the-andersons/, accessed 15/11/2026. Read the original case study.
