What we supply

Tell us the material, the machine and the finish you need, and we will propose suitable items.

  • Abrasives

    Sanding discs, sheets and belts for grinding, deburring and surface preparation.

  • Cutting tools for plastic mould making

    Cutting tools for machining mould steel and electrodes in plastic injection mould making.

  • Polishing supplies

    Polishing compound, wax, liquid and buffing wheels for moulds and metal parts. See the polishing group below.

  • Other workshop consumables

    Related consumables on request, quoted case by case.

Polishing group

Tell us the material, the surface finish you need and the machine or hand tool you use; we will propose the right combination.

  • Polishing compound

    Paste or bar with a fine abrasive, used with a buffing wheel or polishing tool to bring moulds and metal parts to a shine.

  • Polishing wax

    Wax bars applied to a buffing wheel, mainly for the final shine.

  • Polishing liquid

    Liquid with a fine abrasive, for machine or hand polishing.

  • Buffing wheels

    Cloth or felt wheels for grinders and polishing machines, used with wax or compound.
