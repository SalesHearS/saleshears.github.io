About HearS

An industrial engineering and technical solutions company

HearS supplies industrial weighing systems and electrical materials in Vietnam. Its engineering team delivered the projects shown on this site.

Request a quoteContact us

Who we are

CÔNG TY TNHH HEARS is a Vietnamese company based in Ho Chi Minh City, with a branch in Dak Lak and an office in Da Nang. HearS supplies tank weighing systems, off-grid farm silo weighing, truck weighing by project, electrical insulation and cable protection materials, and abrasives and polishing supplies for machine and mould shops.

Our approach is simple: understand the application, specify what fits, supply it with the right documents, and stay available after delivery.

At a glance

  • Three lines: industrial weighing (including off-grid farm silo weighing), electrical materials, abrasives and polishing supplies
  • Strategic distribution partner in Vietnam of Aerchs
  • An engineering team with 13 completed solar projects
  • Offices in Ho Chi Minh City, Dak Lak and Da Nang

Philosophy and approach

  • Fit first, price second

    We specify equipment for the application first, then discuss quantity and price.

  • Documents you can rely on

    Datasheets and drawings come from the manufacturers, and we say clearly what has been confirmed in writing.

  • Support after delivery

    We stay available for commissioning questions and maintenance planning.

Quality commitment

Quality at HearS is a commitment to process, not a badge. We confirm specifications in writing, we say clearly what a manufacturer has stated, and we do not claim certifications that HearS does not hold.

  • Specifications confirmed in writing before an order
  • Clear separation of manufacturer statements and HearS statements
  • Honest answers when a product is not the right fit

Development direction

We are growing the weighing business with Emery Winslow Scale solutions (Emery Winslow Scale Company, USA), developing electrical materials with our strategic partners, and using the experience of completed projects to give industrial customers engineering support.

  • Industrial weighing, starting with tank and vessel systems
  • Electrical materials for industry
  • Engineering and integration support
  • Long-term partnerships in Vietnam
