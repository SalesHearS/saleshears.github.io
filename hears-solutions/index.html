HearS Solutions

Solutions packaged by HearS. We do the mechanical design, installation, commissioning and integration ourselves, and write MES software for each customer. HearS chooses the equipment for each job, and does the engineering around it.

What HearS packages

  • HearS Integration & Services

    Mechanical design to fit load cells to existing tanks, silos and hoppers; installation; commissioning; integration with PLC, SCADA or ERP; custom MES software; calibration and maintenance support.

    Integration & Services: HearS Integration & Services

  • Off-grid farm feed silo weighing

    Feasibility study with ANDUOR (solar and battery storage partner). Hydrostatic load cells under the silos, a solar cabinet and stock data sent to a phone. Not yet a delivered product.

    Feasibility study: Off-grid farm feed silo weighing

    A solar array drives the pump for coffee, pepper and durian farms far from the grid, with a storage tank and drip lines. Handled by the Dak Lak branch.

    Solar irrigation: Off-grid solar irrigation for upland farms
