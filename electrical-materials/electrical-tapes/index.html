Electrical materials

Electrical tapes

Tapes that insulate, wrap and protect conductors, joints and equipment, from general-purpose to high-temperature and foil types.

Request a quoteHow ordering works

What to know

The right electrical tape depends on the film (such as PVC, polyester or polyimide), the adhesive, the operating temperature and the electrical environment. Tell us the application and we help you narrow the choice. Selected tapes are supplied through our strategic partner Aerchs.

Typical uses

  • Joint and splice insulation

    Insulate and seal connections and splices.

  • Wrapping and bundling

    Wrap harnesses and hold wires together.

  • Heat and shielding

    High-temperature insulation, and foil tapes for shielding and heat spreading.

Choosing the film

  • PVC: general-purpose, flexible insulation
  • Polyester: thin and strong, common in coils and transformers
  • Polyimide: for high-temperature uses
  • Foil tapes: shielding, grounding and heat spreading

What to tell us when you request a quote

  • Film or material, if known
  • Width, thickness and roll length
  • Operating temperature range
  • Adhesive type, such as acrylic, silicone or rubber
  • Voltage or dielectric requirement
  • Quantity and delivery location

Frequently asked questions

Which tape suits high temperature?

Polyimide and PTFE-based tapes are usually considered. Check the continuous temperature rating on the manufacturer's datasheet; we confirm it for your order.

Can you supply custom widths or die-cut shapes?

Ask us. Our partner can quote slitting and die-cutting to your drawing.
