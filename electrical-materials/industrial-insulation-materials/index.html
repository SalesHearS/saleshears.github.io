Industrial insulation materials

Cloth, laminates, boards and sheets for electrical and thermal insulation in industrial equipment.

Request a quoteHow ordering works

What to know

Industrial insulation materials separate, support and protect electrical parts and heat sources. They come as cloth, laminated sheets, boards, silicone and PTFE sheets, cut to size or supplied as rolls. Selected materials are supplied through our strategic partners; ask us which fit your need.

Typical uses

  • Electrical insulation

    Separate live parts in panels and equipment.

  • Thermal barriers

    Protect parts from nearby heat sources.

  • Gaskets and spacers

    Cut parts for sealing, spacing and support.

Specifying the material

  • Material for the temperature and the dielectric requirement
  • Thickness and flatness
  • Cut-to-size parts or rolls
  • Resistance to oil, chemicals and moisture

What to tell us when you request a quote

  • Material, if known
  • Thickness and size, or the drawing for cut parts
  • Operating temperature
  • Dielectric or voltage requirement
  • Environment and chemicals
  • Quantity

Frequently asked questions

Can you cut parts to a drawing?

Send the drawing with your request. We confirm with the manufacturer whether it can be supplied and quote it.

How do I choose the thickness?

Thickness depends on the voltage, the temperature and the mechanical load. Tell us the requirement and we help choose.
