What we collect

When you send a form, we receive what you type: your name, company, email, optional phone and country, the type of inquiry and your message. We also receive the page you sent the form from and the language of the page.

Why we use it

To reply to your inquiry, prepare a quotation and manage our business relationship with you. We do not sell your data.

Legal basis

We process your data with your consent, which you give by ticking the consent box, and for our legitimate interest in answering business inquiries.

Who receives it

Only service providers that help us run the website and email, and our manufacturing partners when your request concerns their products and you agree.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to handle your request and our business relationship. We delete it when it is no longer needed.

Cookies and analytics

The site uses strictly necessary storage. Anonymous visit statistics load only if you accept them. See the cookie policy.

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, or withdraw your consent, by emailing HearSVietnam@gmail.com. Applicable Vietnamese data protection rules apply.
